An editorial demonstration

Tachymeterlily is a design and watch-discovery concept. It is not presented as a manufacturer, authorised dealer, repair workshop or marketplace. There are no claims of company history, awards, certifications or customer endorsements.

Photographs are illustrative

Watch photographs demonstrate visual directions. A visible brand does not indicate a partnership, available inventory or permission to sell that brand. General captions are not authenticated model descriptions. Colour and apparent scale may vary across screens.

Technical guidance has limits

Movement descriptions and the tachymeter example provide general context. Always follow the manual for the particular watch. Do not operate crowns or pushers in circumstances the maker prohibits. No water-resistance or servicing claim is made for a pictured item.

Fit guidance

Suggested case-size ranges are starting points only. Wrist shape, lug length, thickness, strap and personal preference all affect comfort. Trying the actual watch is more informative than a single measurement.
