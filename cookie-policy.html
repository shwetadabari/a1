Cookie policy

Manage optional Google Analytics on Tachymeterlily and learn how the site remembers your cookie choice.

Your choice comes first

The site asks whether you want to allow optional Google Analytics. Rejecting it leaves the collection, navigation, fit guide and enquiry draft available.

What the site stores

The preference tl-analytics is saved in your browser’s localStorage with a yes or no value. It persists until you change it, clear site storage or your browser removes it. This is a preference record, not a contact-form submission.

Google Analytics

After acceptance, Google’s tag G-0LY0HY7L01 loads and can set first-party analytics cookies, including _ga and property-related cookies. These help distinguish visits and measure interaction. Exact cookie names and lifetimes depend on Google’s current implementation and property configuration; inspect your browser for the active values.

Changing a preference

Use the button below to reopen the choice panel. To stop an already loaded tag, reject analytics and reload the page. Delete previously set analytics cookies using your browser settings if you want to remove them immediately.

Other requests

Remote images and fonts may contact their providers to display the page. They are separate from the optional Analytics tag. Hosting access controls may also use their own necessary storage or cookies.
