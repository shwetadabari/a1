The watch guide

Worth asking.

A concise guide to the details that matter before choosing and wearing a watch.

What does a tachymeter measure?

A tachymeter scale converts elapsed time over a known distance into an average rate. For one unit of distance, the rate is 3,600 divided by elapsed seconds. Follow the instructions for the particular watch.

How do I choose the right case size?

Treat diameter as a starting point. Lug-to-lug length, thickness and strap shape also affect fit. A watch should sit securely without the lugs projecting beyond the top of your wrist.

Are these watches available to purchase?

This is an editorial demonstration, not a live store. Photographs illustrate watch styles; no inventory, prices, brand affiliation or checkout is offered.

Does water resistance mean waterproof?

No. Use the manufacturer’s instructions for your model and activity. Seals can deteriorate, and crowns or pushers may have specific operating restrictions.

What is the difference between quartz and mechanical?

A quartz watch uses an electronic oscillator and a power source. A mechanical watch stores energy in a spring; automatic versions can wind that spring through wrist movement. Maintenance depends on the model.
