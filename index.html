06 / Behind the name

A measure
of movement.

A tachymeter relates elapsed time to a rate over a known distance. Lily introduces the counterpoint: an appreciation for form that needs no calculation.

One distance unit · Thirty seconds
3,600 ÷ 30 = 120

An average rate of 120 distance units per hour. An illustrative calculation, not an instruction for operating any particular watch.

10 / Before you decide

A few good
questions.

What does a tachymeter measure?

A tachymeter scale converts elapsed time over a known distance into an average rate. For one unit of distance, the rate is 3,600 divided by elapsed seconds. Follow the instructions for the particular watch.

How do I choose the right case size?

Treat diameter as a starting point. Lug-to-lug length, thickness and strap shape also affect fit. A watch should sit securely without the lugs projecting beyond the top of your wrist.
