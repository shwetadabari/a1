Privacy policy

Read how this Tachymeterlily demonstration handles enquiry drafts, optional analytics and browser preferences.

Scope

This policy describes this Tachymeterlily demonstration as configured on 28 September 2026. It covers the pages on this site, the local enquiry draft and optional analytics. No customer account, payment service or mailing list is provided.

Information you enter

The contact form uses your name, email, interest and message to produce a draft in the current page. The site code does not send those fields to a server or save them in browser storage. Closing or refreshing the page may remove the draft. Please do not enter payment details or sensitive personal information.

Optional analytics

When you accept analytics, the site loads Google Analytics using measurement ID G-0LY0HY7L01. Google may process page interactions, browser or device information and online identifiers according to the configured Analytics property and its own policies. Analytics does not load through this site’s code before your acceptance.

Browser preferences and hosting

Your analytics choice is stored under tl-analytics in localStorage so the banner can remember your selection. Hosting and access providers may separately process network request information needed to serve and protect the site. External font and image providers receive requests when their resources load.

Your choices

Use Cookie settings on the cookie policy page to change future analytics loading. You can clear this site’s browser storage and cookies through your browser. Rejecting analytics does not prevent navigation or use of the local draft form. Cookies already set may need to be deleted in your browser.

Operational contact

The displayed address, phone and .example email are fictional demonstration details. A verified operator and working privacy contact must be supplied before this concept is operated as a real business. This demonstration does not promise a response through those details.
